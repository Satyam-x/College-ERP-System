Privacy Policy

Introduction

We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how our ERP system collects, uses, stores, and safeguards data.

Information We Collect

  • User Information: Name, email, phone number, and login credentials.
  • Business Data: Financial records, inventory details, employee information, and other operational data entered into the ERP.
  • System Usage Data: Logs, device information, and IP addresses for security and performance monitoring.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve ERP services.
  • To ensure secure access and prevent unauthorized use.
  • To generate reports and analytics for business operations.
  • To communicate updates, support, and notifications.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Information may be shared only in these cases:

  • With trusted third-party service providers (e.g., hosting, backup, payment gateways).
  • When required by law or regulatory authorities.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users and systems.

Data Security

  • Encryption of sensitive data during transmission and storage.
  • Role-based access control to limit data exposure.
  • Regular audits and monitoring to detect and prevent breaches.

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as required by law. Upon termination, data may be archived or securely deleted based on compliance requirements.

Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Request a copy of your stored data.
  • Withdraw consent for specific data processing activities.

Cookies & Tracking

Our ERP may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze system performance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated through the ERP system or official channels.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@yourcompany.com.
