Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" at the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

Can I export reports?

Yes, most reports can be exported in PDF or Excel format. Look for the "Export" button on the report page.

How do I add a new user?

Admins can add new users by navigating to Settings → User Management and clicking "Add User".
